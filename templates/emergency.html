Why O- in an MCI?

According to the compatibility model used in this project, O- can be given to all supported blood types. This makes it especially important during a mass casualty incident when blood may be required urgently.

Effect on Routine Blood Issue Strategy

O- is a relatively rare blood type in the population distribution used by the system. Therefore, during routine blood issue, the system prefers suitable, more common compatible alternatives when possible and attempts to preserve O- for emergency situations.
